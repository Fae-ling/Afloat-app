Flight status

With this key, typing a flight number into a plan fills in the time and the airport, and on the day Afloat shows delays, the terminal, check-in desks and the gate, or a cancellation. The data comes from AeroDataBox, through RapidAPI (a marketplace for these services). It takes about five minutes, once.

What it costs

The Basic plan is free and usually needs no card. It allows a few hundred lookups a month, which is plenty: Afloat asks about a flight when you look it up and a few times on the day before and the day itself.

The free plan has a hard limit, so it can never charge you. If it runs out in a busy month, flight details simply stop until the next month.

1. Make a RapidAPI account

  1. Open AeroDataBox on RapidAPI.
  2. Sign up (an email address or your Google account is enough).

2. Choose the free plan

  1. On the AeroDataBox page, open Pricing.
  2. Under Basic ($0), tap Subscribe. Don’t pick a paid plan.

3. Copy the key

  1. Open the Endpoints (or Playground) tab of AeroDataBox.
  2. In the example request, find X-RapidAPI-Key and copy the value next to it: a long mix of letters and numbers.
  3. It’s your RapidAPI key, the same for every service there, so treat it like a password.

Add it to Afloat

  1. In Afloat, open Settings (top right) → Connections, and scroll to Travel services for Plans.
  2. Under Flight status, paste the key and tap Save. Afloat checks it with one small request, then it works for everyone in your household.
Afloat keeps the key on its server, where only Afloat can use it. Your phones, the other people in your household and other households never see it; Connections only shows its last four characters. To stop, tap Remove in Connections. Only the household’s owner can add or remove keys.

Using it in a plan

  1. In a plan, under Getting there, set the journey to Flight.
  2. Type the flight number (like OK652 or FR 1234) and tap Look it up. Afloat fills in the time, the airport and “be there 2 hours early”; change any of it under the journey.
  3. Or paste the booking email with ✉ Add a booking: the flight number comes along by itself.

If something doesn’t work

  1. “didn’t accept that key”: check you subscribed to the Basic plan (step 2); a key without a plan is refused.
  2. “No flight found that day”: check the number and the journey’s date. Very far-off flights may not be scheduled yet.
  3. “busy (too many asks)”: the month’s free allowance is used up. It comes back on the 1st.
