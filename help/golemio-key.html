Prague public transport: live delays

With this key, a train, bus or tram in one of your plans shows whether it’s on time, late or cancelled, from three hours before you go. It covers Prague and Central Bohemia (PID). The key comes from Golemio, Prague’s open data platform. It’s free and takes about five minutes, once.

What it costs

Nothing. Golemio’s keys are free, with no card and no plan to choose. Afloat only asks about the departures in your plans, a handful of times on the day, far below any limit.

1. Make an account

  1. Open api.golemio.cz/api-keys. The page is in Czech; your browser can translate it if you like.
  2. Register with your email address and a password.
  3. Open the email Golemio sends you and confirm your address with the link in it.

2. Make the key

  1. Go back to api.golemio.cz/api-keys and sign in.
  2. Use the button that generates a new API key.
  3. Copy the whole key. It’s long (a few hundred characters, with dots in it), so copy it with the copy button or select all of it; a missing piece is the usual reason it fails.

Add it to Afloat

  1. In Afloat, open Settings (top right) → Connections, and scroll to Travel services for Plans.
  2. Under Prague public transport, paste the key and tap Save. Afloat checks it with one small request, then it works for everyone in your household.
Afloat keeps the key on its server, where only Afloat can use it. Your phones, the other people in your household and other households never see it; Connections only shows its last four characters. To stop, tap Remove in Connections. Only the household’s owner can add or remove keys.

Using it in a plan

  1. In a plan, under Getting there, set the journey to Train, bus or tram.
  2. Fill in the stop you leave from (as it’s called on the timetable, e.g. Smíchovské nádraží), the line (S7, 22, R9) and the time it leaves.
  3. On the day, Afloat shows “on time”, “8 min late” or “cancelled” on Today, with your own “if the train is cancelled” plan when there is one.

If something doesn’t work

  1. “didn’t accept that key”: the key was probably cut short when copying. Copy it again, all of it.
  2. “isn’t on the board (yet)”: check the stop’s name matches the timetable, and the line and time. The board only reaches a few hours ahead, so it fills in on the day.
  3. Outside Prague and Central Bohemia: Golemio only knows PID. Elsewhere, Afloat uses the time you planned.
